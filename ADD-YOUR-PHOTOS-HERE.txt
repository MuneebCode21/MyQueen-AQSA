Put your photos in this folder.

The site is already looking for these exact filenames (you can rename them,
just make sure the "src" in js/script.js matches whatever you use):

  hero.jpg        -> the big photo at the very top of index.html
  final.jpg       -> the photo shown at the very end, on final.html
  photo-01.jpg    -> memories.html scrapbook
  photo-02.jpg       "
  photo-03.jpg       "
  photo-04.jpg       "
  photo-05.jpg       "
  photo-06.jpg       "
  photo-07.jpg       "
  photo-08.jpg       "
  photo-09.jpg       "
  photo-10.jpg       "
  story-01.jpg    -> story.html timeline
  story-02.jpg       "
  story-03.jpg       "
  story-04.jpg       "
  story-05.jpg       "

Until a file exists, the site shows a soft placeholder frame with the
expected filename printed on it — nothing breaks, nothing looks like an
error. Just drop the real photo in with the matching name and refresh.

You can also add more/fewer photos than the defaults above — see the
"memories" and "timeline" lists near the top of js/script.js, and the
README.md in the project root for the full guide.

Delete this file once you don't need it any more — it has no effect on
the website itself.
